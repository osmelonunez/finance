# Finance v3.7.0 - Category Budgets

This release adds a top-level **Budgets** workspace for comparing expected monthly spending with actual spending in each category.

## Effective budget

- Each category can have an effective monthly budget.
- Budgets can only be edited from the current month.
- The latest configured budget automatically continues into subsequent months.
- Changing the current budget does not modify the amounts applied to previous months.
- Categories continue to be created, edited, and deleted from their independent workspace.
- New categories automatically appear as `Without budget`.
- A confirmed action removes the budget from the current month without modifying history.
- A removed budget can be assigned again later.
- Demo data forces an initial budget for every category, including categories explicitly left without a budget.
- Clearing demo data restores the previous state of reactivated categories and removes budgets created exclusively by the demo.

## Monthly tracking

- Summary of total budget, actual spending, remaining amount, and categories at risk or over budget.
- Consumption bars for each category.
- Visual alerts at 80%, 90%, and 100%.
- Filters for all, at-risk, exceeded, and unbudgeted categories.
- Actual spending calculated from expense records whose source is monthly.
- Direct link from Budgets to the independent category management workspace for administrators and editors.

## History

- Navigation through previous months.
- Historical months are read-only.
- Each month shows the latest budget that was effective at that time.
- Budget-versus-actual variance, including the amount by which a category was exceeded.
- Future months are unavailable.
- Quick access back to the current month.

## Dashboard

- Budget summary for the selected month.
- Actual spending compared with the total budget.
- Number of categories that exceeded their budget.
- Direct link to the monthly Budgets detail.

## Reports

- New top-level `Reports` workspace.
- Eight email styles, adding Receipt, Statement and Magazine, independently selectable for monthly and yearly reports.
- Template previews use the selected period's real data and the same renderer used for email delivery.
- Month, quarter and year comparisons through two dropdowns generated from the configured year window.
- Income, expense, saving and balance indicators with current/previous values and absolute and percentage changes.
- Income, expense, saving and balance evolution chart for the last 6 or 12 months and the configured year window.
- Controls can show or hide each series without reloading the report.
- One selector for all valid Free, MoM and YoY combinations, with automatic reference-period selection.
- Category breakdown, new/stopped-spending detection and top-five increase/decrease rankings.
- Shared category, bank, account, card and loan filters across Summary, Comparisons and Evolution.
- Contextual CSV export and a purpose-built print/save-as-PDF view.
- Per-user saved reports preserve sections, periods, modes and filters.
- Monthly and yearly income, expense, saving, and balance views.
- Visual spending breakdown by category.
- Top expenses table for the selected period.
- Monthly and yearly email report settings.
- Recent email delivery history and status.
- SMTP remains an independent technical setting under Management.

## Data and compatibility

- New `018_category_budgets.sql`, `019_budget_disabled_state.sql`, and `020_saved_reports.sql` migrations.
- Budgets retain their category, effective month, amount, and audit fields.
- The disabled state explicitly records that a category has no budget, preventing an older amount from being inherited again.
- Category and effective month form a unique combination.
- Spending amounts are not duplicated: actual spending is calculated from `records`.
- Categories with movements or budget history cannot be deleted.
- The version displayed in the footer is updated to `3.7.0`.
